# Engineering reliable digital systems for complex information workflows

I build privacy-aware and traceable digital systems for complex information and operational workflows, with a focus on information security, controlled automation, and evidence-based system behavior.

Current work focuses on legal information infrastructure, controlled scholarly publishing automation, and evidence-oriented project-state tooling.

## Design priorities

- information-security and privacy boundaries by design
- explicit provenance and traceability
- deterministic processing where it improves reliability
- controlled automation with clear human decision boundaries
- conservative handling of uncertainty and failure states
- maintainable integration with existing systems and workflows
